Abstract
We present a proposed neutral real scalar formulation within an extended Tanfarid Quantum
Thermodynamic Universe (TQTU) programme, termed the Newtonfarid Master Equation. A
canonical scalar field coupled linearly to prescribed inertial mass density yields an attractive
Yukawa pair potential, reducing to an inverse-distance potential in its massless static limit.
With the normalization used here, the effective strength is G
eff
= g²/(4π) in natural units. The
factor 1/2 in the total on-shell source energy cancels between the two cross terms of a
two-source configuration. Universality is assumed through the common mass-proportional
source and is not derived from microscopic matter dynamics. We distinguish the static
susceptibility from the equal-time quantum correlator, explain the periodic zero-mode
prescription, and separate analytical identities from author-reported numerical observations.
The original CPL rotor phase sector has not been shown to generate the added neutral scalar
or its coupling. The construction is therefore a conditional effective model with a Newtonian
correspondence limit, not a completed microscopic derivation or an experimentally
established law.

protecting-symmetry calculation remains necessary. The phrase “shift-protected massless
